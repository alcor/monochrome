Brown butter oat cookies

These cookies take 15 minutes of work and 12 minutes in the oven. Browning the butter first is the one step that cannot be skipped: it removes water, which keeps the edges crisp, and it adds the toasted flavor that plain melted butter does not have.

The recipe makes 24 cookies. The dough keeps for 3 days in the refrigerator and for 2 months in the freezer, and it bakes better after a night of rest because the oats have time to take up moisture.

Ingredients

  • 225 g unsalted butter
  • 200 g dark brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 180 g plain flour
  • 240 g rolled oats
    • not instant oats, which turn to paste
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda and 1 teaspoon flaky salt

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a light-colored pan over medium heat and keep cooking, stirring, until it smells of nuts and the solids turn brown, about 6 minutes. Pour it into a bowl and let it cool for 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk the sugar into the butter, then the eggs, one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour, oats, baking soda and salt until no dry flour shows.
  4. Scoop 24 balls onto two lined trays and bake at 180°C for 12 minutes, turning the trays once.

Notes from testing

We baked 9 batches. Batches chilled overnight spread 15% less than batches baked at once, and a tray baked at 190°C burned at the edges before the centers set.
